OVERVIEW

What the EB-1A actually is.

The EB-1A is the first-preference employment green card for people with extraordinary ability in the sciences, arts, education, business or athletics. It's reserved for the small share of people who have risen to the very top of their field.

Unlike most employment categories, you file it for yourself. There's no job offer to secure and no labor market test to pass — you only need to show that you'll keep working in your field once you're in the US.

USCIS reviews it in two steps. First, does the evidence meet at least three of ten regulatory criteria? Then, taken as a whole, does it show sustained national or international acclaim? Most petitions are won or lost in the second step.
